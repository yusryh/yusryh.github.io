I came out in May 2006, so it has been almost 20 years. I've never been really out there, but I also did not hide. I realised I was interested in males in my early school years. Perhaps being in all-boys school for my first 10 years of schooling played a part, but I distinctly remember finding the character Michael Knight from Knight Rider being attractive, which would have preceded my school years.

Despite being out, I didn't date much or having anything I would consider a signification relationship. Perhaps it is because I've never been in the scene. Never liked crowds, never like being up late, never liked loud music, so I never actually went to gay clubs or any sort. Closest was probably Mardi Gras in Sydney, which was in Feb/March just before my coming out, but that was more for the eye candy and just experiencing the atmosphere. So until 2011, I didn't really explore the gay scene until I started using Grindr.

Grindr was an eye-opening experience. All the gay and bi guys in one list. But I soon realised Grindr was just full of people wanting hookups and I found it frustratingly hard to use to find someone who was more interested in dating and relationships. The character limit also made it hard to write a good detailed profile and thus also made hard for me to find people that would be interesting to talk to. I'm not much of a communicator, so I'd prefer to read to find a potential match, rather than having to spend time chatting to find that initial match. But I pushed on, and also started using Jack'D. Through these apps, I went on several dates, but none of them lasted long enough for me to consider a long term relationship. But it was nice.

Looking back, I also found that maintaining a relationship where you are not 100% interested in a person is super hard. The effort that goes into dating was too much for me as I realised I wasn't a very traditional person. To me dates would be like going for a walk, run, hike, or a swim and having a meal after that; or perhaps going to a museum or exhibition. I wasn't into movies or concerts, or traditional dinner dates, and I wasn't much of a talker. I realise I just like being in the presence of a person and just enjoying the time together quietly - hugs, cuddles and kisses included. The fact that the kind of person I would be interested would also be considered a clone of me was another reason why it was hard to find a good match. I'm interested in people around my size and age, with fairly similar interests - technology, software engineering, travel, exercise - and sexual kinks - I wanted someone who was versatile and equally adept at being a dom or sub, and somewhat kinky.

So after my last relationship, which was around 2018, I stopped trying to find someone. I was still browsing eye-candy on Grindr, and JackD, but I didn't actively approached or talked to anyone on these applications. Anyway, I got busy running my own start-ups and decided to focus my energy on them instead.

Back to the present, I started watching Heated Rivalry, and after episode 6, which is the last one, I'm starting to think about exploring the gay scene again. Maybe be being a little more out there. Perhaps with my generation being in our 40s, I would be able to find more homebodies around my age and that could potentially make dating easier for me. I don't really have an outwardly look that screams gay, but I have started wearing a black rainbow nylon bracelet with my Garmin watch. Let's see where 2026 brings; it would be nice to find someone who likes me for who I am and I that I like him back for who he is.
