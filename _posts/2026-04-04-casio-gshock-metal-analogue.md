So here is a release from Casio that I've just recently realised. They have this cool looking analogue-only metal G-Shock watch that has Bluetooth-based sync. Pretty limited in connected functions including not having a step-tracker, but still a cool looking watch. I really dig this sky blue version.

More details can be found at [Casio Germany](https://www.casio.com/de/watches/gshock/product.GMC-B2100AD-2A/). Might try to get it when I visit Japan. G-Shocks and many Japanese brands tend to be cheaper in Japan.
